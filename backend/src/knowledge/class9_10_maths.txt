MATHEMATICS KNOWLEDGE BASE — CLASS 9 & 10 (Odisha Board)
=========================================================

=== NUMBER SYSTEMS ===
Natural numbers: 1, 2, 3, ... Whole numbers: 0, 1, 2, 3, ... Integers: ...-2, -1, 0, 1, 2... Rational numbers: can be expressed as p/q where p and q are integers and q≠0. All terminating decimals and repeating decimals are rational. Irrational numbers cannot be expressed as p/q — examples: √2, √3, π, e. Real numbers include all rational and irrational numbers. Euclid's division lemma: for any two positive integers a and b, there exist unique q and r such that a = bq + r, where 0 ≤ r < b. HCF (Highest Common Factor) using Euclid's algorithm: repeatedly apply division lemma until remainder is zero. LCM × HCF = product of two numbers (for any two numbers).

=== POLYNOMIALS ===
A polynomial p(x) = a_n x^n + a_{n-1} x^{n-1} + ... + a_1 x + a_0. Degree is the highest power. Zero of a polynomial p(x) is a value k such that p(k) = 0. Remainder theorem: when p(x) is divided by (x-a), remainder = p(a). Factor theorem: (x-a) is a factor of p(x) if and only if p(a) = 0. For a quadratic ax²+bx+c=0: sum of roots = -b/a, product of roots = c/a. Discriminant D = b²-4ac. If D>0: two distinct real roots. If D=0: two equal roots. If D<0: no real roots. Quadratic formula: x = (-b ± √(b²-4ac)) / 2a.

=== COORDINATE GEOMETRY ===
The coordinate plane has x-axis (horizontal) and y-axis (vertical). A point is (x, y). Distance formula: d = √[(x2-x1)² + (y2-y1)²]. Section formula (internal): point dividing AB in ratio m:n = ((mx2+nx1)/(m+n), (my2+ny1)/(m+n)). Midpoint = ((x1+x2)/2, (y1+y2)/2). Slope of a line m = (y2-y1)/(x2-x1) = tan(θ). Slope-intercept form: y = mx + c. Standard form: ax + by + c = 0. Two lines are parallel if their slopes are equal. Two lines are perpendicular if the product of their slopes = -1.

=== TRIANGLES AND GEOMETRY ===
Congruence criteria: SSS, SAS, ASA, AAS, RHS. Similarity criteria: AA, SSS, SAS. For similar triangles, ratio of areas = square of ratio of corresponding sides. Pythagoras theorem: in a right-angled triangle, (hypotenuse)² = (base)² + (perpendicular)². Converse: if a²+b²=c², then the angle opposite c is 90°. Properties of a triangle: angle sum = 180°. Exterior angle = sum of two non-adjacent interior angles. Median divides triangle into two equal areas. Centroid divides each median in ratio 2:1 from vertex.

=== CIRCLES ===
Chord: a line segment with both endpoints on the circle. Diameter is the longest chord. Perpendicular from centre to a chord bisects the chord. Equal chords are equidistant from the centre. Tangent to a circle is perpendicular to the radius at the point of contact. From an external point, two tangents are equal in length. Angle in a semicircle = 90°. Angles subtended by the same arc at the circumference are equal. Angle at centre = 2 × angle at circumference (same arc).

=== AREAS AND VOLUMES ===
Triangle area = ½ × base × height. Heron's formula: area = √[s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)] where s = (a+b+c)/2. Rectangle: area = l×b, perimeter = 2(l+b). Circle: area = πr², circumference = 2πr. Cylinder: volume = πr²h, curved surface area = 2πrh, total SA = 2πr(h+r). Cone: volume = ⅓πr²h, slant height l = √(r²+h²), CSA = πrl, total SA = πr(l+r). Sphere: volume = (4/3)πr³, surface area = 4πr². Hemisphere: volume = (2/3)πr³, CSA = 2πr², total SA = 3πr².

=== STATISTICS AND PROBABILITY ===
Mean (ungrouped) = sum of all values / number of values. Mean (grouped) = Σ(fi×xi) / Σfi. Median: the middle value when data is arranged in order. Mode: the value that appears most frequently. For grouped data: Mode = l + [(f1-f0) / (2f1-f0-f2)] × h where l = lower limit of modal class, f1 = frequency of modal class, f0 = frequency of class before, f2 = frequency of class after, h = class width. Probability = number of favourable outcomes / total number of outcomes. P(event) is always between 0 and 1. P(A) + P(not A) = 1. For two mutually exclusive events: P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B).

=== TRIGONOMETRY ===
In a right-angled triangle with angle θ: sin θ = opposite/hypotenuse, cos θ = adjacent/hypotenuse, tan θ = opposite/adjacent = sinθ/cosθ. Reciprocals: cosec θ = 1/sinθ, sec θ = 1/cosθ, cot θ = 1/tanθ. Identities: sin²θ + cos²θ = 1, 1 + tan²θ = sec²θ, 1 + cot²θ = cosec²θ. Standard values: sin 0°=0, sin 30°=1/2, sin 45°=1/√2, sin 60°=√3/2, sin 90°=1. cos 0°=1, cos 30°=√3/2, cos 45°=1/√2, cos 60°=1/2, cos 90°=0. tan 0°=0, tan 30°=1/√3, tan 45°=1, tan 60°=√3, tan 90°=undefined. Angle of elevation: the angle measured upward from horizontal to a line of sight above. Angle of depression: measured downward from horizontal to a line of sight below.

=== ARITHMETIC PROGRESSIONS ===
An AP is a sequence where the difference between consecutive terms is constant (common difference d). General term: a_n = a + (n-1)d. Sum of n terms: S_n = n/2 × [2a + (n-1)d] or S_n = n/2 × (first term + last term). If three numbers are in AP, their sum = 3 times the middle number.

=== REAL-WORLD PROBLEM TIPS ===
Read the problem carefully. Identify what is given and what to find. Draw a diagram for geometry problems. Write the formula before substituting numbers. Check units — if the question uses metres, your answer should be in metres or metres squared or metres cubed. Always verify your answer makes logical sense (e.g., a negative length is impossible).
